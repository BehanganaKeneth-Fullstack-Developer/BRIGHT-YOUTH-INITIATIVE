Our community

“The journey to a bright future begins when we choose to invest in young people.”

Bright Future Youth Initiative began in 2018 when young professionals saw talented youth in their communities without access to skills training, mentorship or start-up support.

Today, our programmes reach out-of-school youth, recent graduates without jobs, and young women and girls in low-income communities across Kampala and Wakiso.
